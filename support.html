Support

Need help with Cardavela? Open a request on our support page — describe the problem, your iPhone/iPad model and iOS version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Quick answers

How do I study?

Open My cards and tap the start button, or start any deck in the Library. Tap the card to flip it, then swipe right (Know), left (Don't know) or up (Easy), or use the three buttons.

How do I download or delete a deck?

Library → Deck store → Download. To delete, tap “…” on a deck in My decks. Your answers stay in statistics.

How do I import my words?

Library → “+” → Import a deck. Choose an Anki .apkg or a TXT/CSV list with one pair per line: word, tab or “;” or “ - ”, translation.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings → Backup → Export backup, then Restore backup on the new device. A normal iPhone backup also keeps the app data.

A word is not spoken

Install an English or Russian voice in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
